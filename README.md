## 📖 What Is Quasimorph?

**Quasimorph** is a dark, high-stakes turn-based extraction RPG that blends cyberpunk, cosmic body horror, and PMCs trying to profit from the Apocalypse[citation:5][citation:9]. After three years in Early Access, the game launched its full 1.0 version on July 30, 2026[citation:1][citation:5].

You play as a hardened PMC fighter in a world where space has been privatized and corporations are stretching their claws far beyond the Solar System[citation:9]. Complete corporate contracts, and you keep everything you manage to extract. Die, and you lose everything you brought with you. But you can always print a new mercenary and try the contract again[citation:9].

**Key Game Features**:
- 👾 Turn-based extraction roguelike with cyberpunk horror themes
- 💀 Permadeath with clone mechanics — die and lose everything
- 💰 Corporate contracts and reputation system
- 🧬 Deep augmentation and body modification system
- 🔫 Hundreds of weapons, armor pieces, and gadgets
- 🛸 Ship management and resource recovery
- 📖 1.0 release adds two full storylines (RealWare and AnCom) with 2 endings each[citation:5]
- 🔫 29 new guns, 2 new damage types, deployable covers, gas grenades[citation:5]
